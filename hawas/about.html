Our Mission

Hawas is your passport to the world of specialty coffee. We believe that exploring coffee should be as exciting as the drink itself—fun, rewarding, and deeply personal.

The name "Hawas" (حواس) comes from Arabic, meaning "senses" or "passion." It perfectly captures what we're about: engaging all your senses in the discovery of exceptional coffee while fueling your passion for exploration.

Our app transforms the way you interact with coffee. Instead of just drinking it, you collect it. Instead of forgetting what you tried, you build a visual library of your coffee journey. Every scan unlocks a new card, every card tells a story.

Our Story

Hawas was born from a simple frustration: standing in a coffee shop, staring at bags of beans, and having no idea what to try next. Which roaster was good? What did "natural process" mean? Was Ethiopian coffee really that different from Colombian?

We realized that specialty coffee had a discovery problem. There was too much information scattered across too many places, and no fun way to track what you'd already tried or what you loved.

So we built Hawas—a gamified coffee encyclopedia that makes learning about coffee as addictive as drinking it. Part collection game, part coffee journal, part discovery engine.

How Hawas Works

Scan & Discover: Point your camera at any coffee bag. Our AI uses OCR and vision technology to identify the product instantly—no manual entry needed.

Unlock Cards: When you scan a new coffee, you unlock its digital trading card. Cards come in three rarity levels: Common, Rare, and the coveted Golden cards.

Build Your Collection: View all your discovered coffees in a beautiful card grid. Filter by origin, roaster, flavor notes, or rarity. Add personal notes and memories to each card.

Complete Journeys: Embark on guided coffee adventures like "Ethiopian Explorer" or "Processing Master." Complete milestones to earn special rewards.

Earn Achievements: From your first scan to finding your 50th rare card, there's always something new to unlock.
